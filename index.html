Frequently Asked Questions

What is LionTrack?

LionTrack is a team management app designed specifically for grassroots youth football coaches. It helps you manage squads, track live match stats, record training attendance, award player XP and badges, and share updates with parents.

Is LionTrack free?

LionTrack has a free tier that lets you manage one team with up to 10 players and 3 matches. The Pro subscription unlocks unlimited players, matches, media gallery, season export, and more.

What is Match To Pro?

Match To Pro is an optional player subscription that unlocks professional player comparisons. Players can pick a favourite pro and see how their stats compare, plus get club news and match results from their favourite team.

Can parents see their child's progress?

Yes! Parents link to their child's profile and get a read-only dashboard showing XP, badges, match highlights, attendance, and streaks. Coaches control what's visible.

Is my data secure?

All data is stored securely in Firebase with encryption in transit and at rest. Photos are stored in Firebase Storage with per-coach quotas. You can request full data deletion at any time.

Can I have a secondary coach?

Yes. You can invite a secondary coach who gets read-only access to your team data. They can view everything but can't edit, delete, or create content.
