Support

Getting Started

  • Create an account — Sign up with email/password, Google, or Apple Sign-In.
  • Create your team — Enter your team name and age group to get started.
  • Add players — Add up to 10 players on the free tier, or unlimited with Pro.
  • Run a match — Tap "Start Match" to begin live stat tracking on matchday.

Account & Subscription

  • Free tier — 1 team, up to 10 players, 3 matches. Full access to core features.
  • Pro subscription — Unlimited players, matches, teams, media gallery, season export, and more.
  • Restore purchases — Go to Settings > Subscription > Restore Purchases.
  • Cancel subscription — Manage your subscription through the App Store (iOS) or Google Play (Android).
  • Delete account — Contact us at the email below. All data will be permanently deleted within 30 days.

Troubleshooting

  • App won't load — Check your internet connection. LionTrack requires connectivity for authentication and data sync.
  • Match data not saving — Ensure you have an active Pro subscription if you've exceeded the free tier limits.
  • Notifications not working — Check that notification permissions are enabled in your device settings.
  • Biometric login issues — If you've re-enrolled your fingerprint/face, disable and re-enable biometrics in Settings.
  • Photos not uploading — Check storage quota in Settings. Each coach has a 500MB limit.

Parent & Player Accounts

  • Linking to a child — Parents sign up and enter the link code provided by the coach.
  • Player dashboard — Players see their own XP, badges, streaks, and positive stats only.
  • Match To Pro — An optional player subscription for comparing stats with professional footballers.
