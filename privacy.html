Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2025

Overview

LionTrack ("we", "our", "the app") is developed by Camilleri Creations. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and how we protect it.

We are committed to protecting the privacy of all users, particularly children who use the app through parent or player accounts.

Data We Collect

  • Account information — Name and email address (required for authentication).
  • Team data — Player names, shirt numbers, positions, match statistics, training attendance, and XP/badges.
  • Photos — Optional team media gallery images uploaded by coaches.
  • Audio recordings — Optional safeguarding concern voice notes (encrypted, coach-only access).
  • Device identifiers — Firebase Cloud Messaging token for push notifications.
  • Crash logs — Anonymous crash and performance data via Firebase Crashlytics.

How We Use Your Data

  • To provide and maintain the app's core functionality (team management, stats, messaging).
  • To send push notifications about match results and team updates.
  • To process subscription purchases (via RevenueCat and App Store/Google Play).
  • To diagnose crashes and improve app performance.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or sale to third parties.

Data Storage & Security

  • Storage — All data is stored in Google Firebase (Firestore, Storage, Authentication).
  • Encryption in transit — All connections use TLS/SSL encryption.
  • Encryption at rest — Firebase infrastructure provides server-side encryption.
  • Access control — Firestore security rules restrict data access to authenticated team members only.
  • Biometric credentials — Stored in the device's secure enclave (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore). Never transmitted.

Third-Party Services

  • Firebase (Google) — Authentication, database, storage, messaging, crash reporting.
  • RevenueCat — Subscription management. Receives purchase status and anonymous device ID only.
  • Apple / Google — In-app purchase processing via App Store and Google Play.

We do not share personal data with any other third parties.

Children's Privacy

LionTrack is designed for grassroots youth football teams. Player and parent accounts may be used by or on behalf of children.

  • Player accounts show only positive statistics (no negative stats are displayed).
  • No comparative rankings, league tables, or public profiles exist.
  • Photo uploads and media are restricted to authenticated team members only.
  • The app does not contain advertising or links to external social media.

Data Retention & Deletion

  • Data is retained as long as your account is active.
  • Archived seasons are moved to cold storage and retained until manually deleted.
  • You may request full account and data deletion by emailing camillericreations@icloud.com.
  • All personal data will be permanently deleted within 30 days of a verified request.
  • Anonymous crash/performance analytics may be retained indefinitely.

Your Rights

  • Access your data at any time through the app.
  • Export your season data as a PDF.
  • Request correction or deletion of your data.
  • Withdraw consent for optional data collection (photos, audio) at any time.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.
